### **Diagram Features**

1. **Color-coded heatmap**

   * Red 🔴: Critical threats (identity, telepresence)
   * Orange 🟠: Medium risk (FSM, nodes, service mesh)
   * Green 🟢: Low risk (audit, mitigation feedback)

2. **Attack vectors**

   * Dotted lines with labels show spoofing, tampering, session hijack, node impersonation

3. **Mitigation feedback loops**

   * Solid green arrows showing **real-time intervention**, e.g., termination of sessions, FSM enforcement, SOC alerts

4. **Layered architecture**

   * Identity → Transition → Telepresence → Nodes → Audit

5. **STRIDE annotations**

   * Each component shows dominant STRIDE risks
