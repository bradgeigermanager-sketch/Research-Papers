Threat Model: Fraudulent Historical Baseline / Negative Template Attack
Executive Summary

This attack seeks to corrupt the perceived history of a system so that future humans or automated devices make decisions based on fabricated precedent rather than reality. The attacker's objective is not merely to create a single false record, but to establish a false normal operating baseline that influences future actions.

This differs from ordinary fraud because the primary target is often the decision-making process itself, not a specific transaction.

Core Risk Statement

The central risk is that an attacker successfully causes a fabricated historical narrative to become the authoritative source from which future decisions are derived. Once a false baseline is accepted and embedded into automation, analytics, policies, or institutional memory, the resulting decisions can remain distorted long after the original fraudulent records were created.
