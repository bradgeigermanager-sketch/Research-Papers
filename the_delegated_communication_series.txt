# ⭐ **THE DELEGATED COMMUNICATION SERIES**  
### *Six Papers on Intent, Agency, and Responsibility in Human and Machine‑Mediated Communication*

This series consists of **six full papers**, each addressing a different dimension of the same core problem:

> **What happens when a communication is sent in your name, but not reviewed by you?**

Each paper is designed to be:
- independently publishable  
- cross‑referenced with the others  
- theoretically grounded  
- historically informed  
- technologically current  

---
