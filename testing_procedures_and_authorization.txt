Would a bully be likely to say they were just "testing" the smaller child they were bullying?

Yes, a bully is highly likely to use the excuse that they were just "testing" or "pushing the limits" of a smaller child.
In psychology, this is a common rationalization tactic known as minimization or cognitive restructuring. It allows the bully to reframe aggressive behavior as something benign, educational, or even helpful.

Why Bullies Use the "Testing" Excuse

• Shifting Blame to the Victim: By claiming they were just "testing" the child's strength, resilience, or "toughness," the bully implies that any negative outcome is the victim's fault for "failing" the test or being too weak.
• Plausible Deniability: It frames the harassment as a game, an experiment, or a standard social rite of passage. If a teacher or parent intervenes, the bully can say, "I wasn't hurting him, I was just seeing what he would do."
• Masking Malice as Mentorship: In some contexts, older or larger bullies will disguise abuse as a form of "toughening up" the younger child, pretending they are preparing them for the "real world."
