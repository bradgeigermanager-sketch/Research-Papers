                            DIMENSIONS 1 TO 30

GENERAL FORMULAS (Gamma Function & Double Factorial Forms):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let n be the dimension, r be the radius, and pi (~3.1415926535).


Volume of an n-dimension sphere (n-ball):
  V_n(r) = [ pi^(n/2) / Gamma(n/2 + 1) ] * r^n
  For Even n=2k:  V_2k(r) = [ pi^k / k! ] * r^(2k)
  For Odd n=2k+1: V_(2k+1)(r) = [ 2^(k+1) * pi^k / (2k+1)!! ] * r^(2k+1)


Surface Area (Boundary Volume) of an n-dimension sphere ((n-1)-sphere):
  S_n(r) = [ 2 * pi^(n/2) / Gamma(n/2) ] * r^(n-1) = (n / r) * V_n(r)
  For Even n=2k:  S_2k(r) = [ 2 * pi^k / (k-1)! ] * r^(2k-1)
  For Odd n=2k+1: S_(2k+1)(r) = [ 2 * (2*pi)^k / (2k-1)!! ] * r^(2k)


Note on notation:
  k!   = k * (k-1) * ... * 1  (Standard Factorial)
  n!!  = n * (n-2) * ... * 1  (Double Factorial)
===============================================================================


DIMENSION n = 1
------------------------------
  Volume Formula:
    V_1(r) = 2 * r^1
    V_1(r) ≈ 2.0000000000 * r^1  (Length of a line segment)
  Surface Area Formula:
    S_1(r) = 2 * r^0
    S_1(r) ≈ 2.0000000000 * r^0  (Number of boundary endpoints)
