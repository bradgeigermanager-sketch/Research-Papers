\section{Comparative Statics}

Increasing desperation $D$ raises $\alpha(D)$ and lowers $\beta(D)$,
making fabrication more attractive.

Increasing institutional pressure $P$ raises $\delta(P)$, making the
handler more likely to act on thin evidence, which increases the payoff
to fabrication.

High verification capacity $V$ and strong sanction credibility $S_c$
support a high-truth equilibrium.
